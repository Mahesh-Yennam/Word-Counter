# Word & Character Counter Pro — Gamility Tools

A modern, high-speed, client-side text analytics and readability inspection tool. Get instant metrics on word count, character density, reading & speaking times, readability grades, and top keyword distributions.

---

## 🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

All calculations and string transformations are performed **strictly within your local web browser**. Your text, essays, passwords, and sensitive notes are **never** transmitted, uploaded, or stored on external servers or cloud APIs.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Live Real-Time Metrics**: Instant calculation of words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, and paragraphs as you type.
- **Estimated Reading & Speaking Times**: Accurate reading pace (~200 wpm) and conversational speaking speed (~130 wpm).
- **Readability & Grade Level**: Integrated Flesch Reading Ease score with an intuitive difficulty gauge (e.g., Very Easy, Standard, Graduate).
- **Top Keyword Density Analyzer**: Live frequency analysis with percentage distribution bars and an optional common stop-word filter.
- **Text Case Transformer**: Quick one-click transformations for UPPERCASE, lowercase, and Title Case.
- **Autosave Draft Recovery**: Preserves your active draft in browser `localStorage` to guard against accidental refreshes.
- **Quick Action Toolbar**: One-click clipboard copy with visual toast confirmation, sample text loader, and instant canvas clearing.
- **Responsive Dark Design**: Tailored glassmorphism UI styled for desktop, tablet, and mobile displays.
